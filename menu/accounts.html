Other Accounts Help

Other Accounts keeps track of your money outside your budget's account, like savings, cash, or investments. Balance is what's in each account today. The By column also counts what's still coming up on your calendar.

Moving Money In And Out

  • When you add a Hidden or Transfer entry (on the calendar, in Recurring Entries, or with the Quick Entry icon), pick the account under Other Account. The list shows each account's balance today, and the entry is titled with the account's name.
  • The account gets the opposite of the entry's amount: a cost on your calendar adds to the account, and a gain takes from it. Moving $100 into savings is a $100 Transfer cost: In Bank goes down $100, and Savings goes up $100.
  • ⭕️ Transfer changes In Bank too, since the money moves between your budget's account and this one. ✖️ Hidden doesn't, like putting cash into savings.
  • Every Hidden or Transfer entry titled with an account's name counts, even one you typed yourself. Changing, moving, or deleting one changes the account too.
  • Since the money is still yours, the tracker doesn't count these entries as costs or gains. Like transfers, only a tracker row set to count transfers counts them.

Balances

  • An account's balance is its entries added up, so there's no balance to type in. Tap an account to see its latest entries and the ones coming up.
  • To put in what's already in an account, like the $3,000 already in your savings, add a Hidden entry for it: a $3,000 cost with Savings picked under Other Account. In Bank stays the same, and Savings goes up $3,000.
  • If an account ever drifts from the real one, add a Hidden entry for the difference the same way (a cost adds to the account, a gain takes from it).

Edit

  • Tap Edit to add an account (New Account), drag accounts by their ≡ bar, or check accounts and delete them.
  • Savings is always there, so it can't be deleted (🔒), but you can rename it.
  • Tap an account to rename it. Its entries (and tracker aliases) get the new name too. Deleting one leaves its entries on your calendar, but they stop changing an account.
