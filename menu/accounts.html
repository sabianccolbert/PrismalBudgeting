Other Accounts Help

Other Accounts keeps track of your money outside your budget's account, like savings, cash, investments, or a credit card. Balance is what's in each account today. In 6 Months is what it will have six months from today, counting what's still coming: entries on your calendar and in Upcoming, and the ones your recurring entries and connected spreadsheets will add. At the top, the total adds them all up with In Bank (your budget's own account), today and in 6 months.

Moving Money In And Out

  • When you add a Hidden or Transfer entry (on the calendar, in Recurring Entries, or with the Quick Entry icon), pick the account under Other Account (before the amount). Each account is a button with its balance today under its name. The entry keeps its own title, and the account shows after it, like "Payday → Savings".
  • ⭕️ Transfer moves money between your budget's account and this one, so In Bank changes and the account gets the opposite amount. Moving $100 into savings is a $100 Transfer cost: In Bank goes down $100, and Savings goes up $100.
  • ✖️ Hidden happens in the account itself, so In Bank stays the same and the account changes by the same amount. Putting $50 in cash into savings is a $50 Hidden gain (Savings goes up $50); paying $30 straight out of savings is a $30 Hidden cost (Savings goes down $30).
  • Only entries with the account picked under Other Account count. An entry titled with an account's name, with no account picked, is an ordinary entry and doesn't change the account. Changing, moving, or deleting an entry for an account changes the account too.
  • % amounts: with an account picked, tap % next to the amount to make the entry a percent of the account's balance as its day starts, like paying 10% of a credit card's balance. It's worked out again whenever your budget changes (one % entry counts in the balance the next one starts from), and once its day passes it stays what it came to.
  • Entries for an account keep their type everywhere else: a ⭕️ Transfer is still a transfer (it isn't a cost or gain, and only a tracker row set to count transfers counts it), and a ✖️ Hidden one is a cost or gain like any Hidden entry (the day's Gains and Costs show it, and the tracker counts it).

Balances

  • Tap an account to type in what's in it today, like the $3,000 already in your savings. That doesn't add an entry, so your calendar, tracker, and Search stay the same. Money owed, like on a credit card, is a Negative balance: tap Negative before or after typing it.
  • From there, its entries add to it, so changing, moving, or deleting one changes the balance too. The panel also shows its latest entries and the ones coming up.
  • Entries, at the end of each account's row, lists every entry made on it (on the calendar, in History, and in Upcoming), newest first, with what each put in or took out. Tap one to change it, like an entry on a zoomed day.
  • Max (optional, in the account's panel): the most it can hold, like a credit card's limit. Its balance then shows over it, like $500.00/$2000.00.
  • If an account ever drifts from the real one, tap it and type in the real balance.

Interest

  • For a credit card (or savings that earn interest), tap the account, turn on Add interest each month, and type its yearly rate (its APR, like 24.99).
  • Once a month, on the day of the month you turned it on (or last typed its balance), the balance changes by a twelfth of the rate: one below $0 goes further below, and one above $0 grows. Each month's interest shows in the account's entries as % Interest, and counts in its balance and In 6 Months.
  • Typing a new balance or a new rate starts the monthly count again from that day, with the interest so far kept in the balance. Turn the switch off to stop it.

Edit

  • Tap Edit to add an account (New Account), drag accounts by their ≡ bar, or check accounts and delete them. Every budget has Savings and Cash: they can be renamed, but not deleted (they show a 🔒 in Edit).
  • Savings is always there, so it can't be deleted (🔒), but you can rename it.
  • Tap an account to rename it. Its entries (and tracker aliases, connected spreadsheets set up with its name, and goals for it) get the new name too. If that would make two recurring entries the same (the same title, refraction type, and account), it says which, so you can change one first. Deleting an account leaves its entries on your calendar, but they stop changing an account.
