Business Help

The Business page is for a budget its owner turned Business Mode on for (Budgets, Calendars & Spreadsheets, in the Menu, under Budgets). Only that budget has it; your other budgets stay as they are.

Categories

  • Each title can be in one of the business's categories: Revenue, Expenses, Asset Purchases, Assets, or one of its owners (what they put in and took out).
  • Pick one under Business in any entry's window, New Entry, or a recurring entry's window, or under Titles here. Like a wavelength, it's the title's: every entry with that title shares it.
  • An entry counts once its day has passed (days before today), from History and the calendar, each year on its own. Every type counts: Regular, Hidden, and Transfer.

What It Shows

  • Revenue and Expenses add up their titles' entries (a refund in Expenses takes away from them), and Profit is the two together.
  • Asset Purchases and Assets add up theirs, and each owner shows what they put in (gains), took out (costs), and the two together.
  • Each category's entries are listed under it, newest first: tap its name to open or close the list.
  • Pick another year at the top to see that one.

Owners

Add the business's owners by name under Owners, then put the titles of what each puts in or takes out (like their contributions and draws) in their category. Taking an owner out takes their titles out of every category.

These are estimates from your entries, not tax or legal advice.
