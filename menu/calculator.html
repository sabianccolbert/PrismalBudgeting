Paycheck Calculator Help

The Paycheck Calculator figures out a paycheck's take home pay. Setting it up takes some work the first time, but after that it's fast: the boxes keep your last numbers, so next time you only change your hours and tap Calculate.

Setting It Up

  • Get your last two or three paychecks.
  • Anything that's the same on every paycheck is a fixed amount: put it in a box marked $.
  • Anything that changes along with your pay is a percentage: put it in a box marked %. To find it, divide the amount by the pay it comes out of (see How The Math Works below), then multiply by 100.
  • Tap Calculate, then compare the results with a real paycheck. How It Adds Up shows each step, so you can see which number is off.
  • A blank box counts as 0.
  • Tip: you can send an AI assistant a few of your paychecks and a screenshot of this page, and ask it what to put in each box. Cover up personal details, like your Social Security number, first.

How The Math Works

  • Gross: regular hours × base pay, plus overtime and premium hours (paid at base pay × their multipliers), plus taxable allowances.
  • Pre-tax medical, pre-tax retirement, and post-tax deduction percentages come out of gross.
  • FICA comes out of gross minus pre-tax medical.
  • Federal and state taxes come out of gross minus pre-tax medical and pre-tax retirement.
  • Take Home: gross, minus pre-tax deductions, taxes, and post-tax deductions, plus non-taxable reimbursements.
