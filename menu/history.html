History Help

  • History keeps every week after it's over, with the newest week on top. Each day shows its date, its In Bank at the end of that day, and its entries.
  • A week moves here when the next one starts (on Sunday), so your calendar always starts with this week.
  • By then, its recurring entries have turned into unique entries (like ❗️✔️), so changing a recurring entry never changes your History.
  • To add an entry to a day in History, use New Entry on the home page (or your Quick Entry icon) with that day's date. If that day already has an entry with the same title and refraction type, the amounts are added together. History's In Bank amounts don't change, but the tracker and Search count the new entry.
  • The tracker counts the last 365 days, from History and the calendar.

Search

  • 🔎 Search (at the top) is the same as Home's: it finds entries by title in History, the calendar, and Upcoming, and, with a To date after the calendar, the recurring entries still to come (up to two years ahead). It floats over the page; × or Escape closes it.
  • Type a title, then pick All, Costs (only its costs), Gains (only its gains), or Tracker (the title is a tracker row, like Food: it finds what that row counts). Press Enter (or tap + Add Title) for another title, and leave the title blank to find everything.
  • From and To are optional. Tap 📅 to pick a date, or, while a date box glows, tap a day in History to fill it in.
  • Results show newest first, with a total of the checked ones. ⭕️ Transfers start unchecked.
