History Help

  • History keeps every week after it's over, with the newest week on top. Each day shows its date; tap a day to zoom in on it and see its In Bank at the end of that day and its entries. Its round + (top right) opens New Entry with that day in its date box, and its × (or a tap anywhere outside it) zooms back out. A zoomed day is part of the page: scroll or pinch the page, and it moves and zooms with it.
  • Tap an entry on a zoomed day to fix it: change its title, amount, refraction type, or Other Account, move it to another day in History, pick its title's Needs, Wants, or Future (once that card is on, on Bob's page), give it a note (at the bottom, under the buttons; an entry with one is underlined), or delete it. A day in History already happened, so fixing one doesn't change In Bank or your Other Accounts' balances (the tracker and Search count the change). Undo takes it back, like any change.
  • A zoomed day works like one on Home: every day zooms to the same size, as tall as its entries need (up to most of the screen), its entries stay on one line (a long one ends in "...", and tapping it shows it whole), and on a phone, a finger scrolls a day with more entries than fit. Swipe it left or right to slide over to the next day or the day before (on a computer, the → and ← keys); on a phone it moves with your finger, and stays if you let go after only a little. If you've zoomed the whole page in, tapping a day zooms the page back out first (or zooms the day in on what's on your screen), so it fits. Pinch or stretch with two fingers on one day to zoom just that day; two fingers across days zoom the whole page. On a computer, hold the mouse button down on a day to magnify it, and keep holding while you slide across the days.
  • A week moves here when the next one starts (on Sunday), so your calendar always starts with this week.
  • By then, its recurring entries have turned into unique entries (like ❗️✔️), so changing a recurring entry never changes your History.
  • To add an entry to a day in History, tap the day, then its + (or use New Entry on the home page, or your Quick Entry icon, with that day's date). If that day already has an entry with the same title, refraction type, and Other Account, New Entry asks whether to add to it or set it to the new amount (the Quick Entry icon adds to it). History's In Bank amounts don't change, but the tracker and Search count the new entry.
  • The tracker counts the last 365 days, from History and the calendar.

Search

  • Search (beside the tabs) is the same as Home's: it finds entries by title in History, the calendar, and Upcoming, and, through its To date, the recurring entries (up to two years ahead) and connected spreadsheets' entries still to come. It floats over the page; × or Escape closes it.
  • Type a title, then pick All, Costs (only its costs), Gains (only its gains), or Tracker (the title is a tracker row, like Food: it finds what that row counts). The little ? just left of All says the same. Press Enter (or tap + Add Title) for another title, and leave the title blank to find everything.
  • Exact, beside each title: off, it finds every title with what you typed in it (gas finds Gas Station too); on, only that title.
  • From and To are optional. With no To, it looks a month past today (or past From). Tap the calendar button to pick a date, or, while History has a dashed outline around it, tap a day in History to fill it in.
  • Results show newest first, with a total of the checked ones; ones after today come first, under Coming up, apart from today and before. ⭕️ Transfers start unchecked. Tap a result to change it, like an entry on a zoomed day.
