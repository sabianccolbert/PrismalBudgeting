Change Logs Help

Change Logs is a record of every change to your budget, newest first: when it was made (Time), how long it took (Took), what it was (Action), and what it changed (Details). In a budget that's shared, Details also say who made each change (By:). The newest 1,000 are kept.

Undo

  • A change that can be taken back has an Undo at its right. Tap it (and say yes) to put back what was there before that change, on every device: the entries it changed (on the calendar, in History, and in Upcoming), the recurring entries, tracker rows, and Equity accounts it added, changed, or deleted, your goals, Bob's page, connected spreadsheets and calendars, the calculators, and notes. In Bank, the balances, and the tracker's sums are worked out again.
  • It only works while everything that change touched is still as the change left it, so it never erases anything you changed since. Otherwise it says what changed (like a day), and nothing is undone.
  • The Undo is a change of its own, with its own Undo, which puts the change back. A change that was undone says Undone.
  • Searches, Upload A Backup, the daily update, connected spreadsheets and calendars updating themselves (the next read would only bring it back), the Debt Calculator keeping its boxes, and very big changes (like renaming an account across years of History) have no Undo, and only the newest changes keep theirs.
  • Home's Undo and Redo (beside New Entry) take back your newest changes there, one at a time, in the tab they were made in.

Search

Search (at the top) looks through the changes: as you type, it lists the ones with every word you typed in them. × or Escape closes it.
