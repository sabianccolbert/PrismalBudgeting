Recurring Entries Help

Everything that repeats, in three cards: your budget's recurring entries, your Calendar Fractals' entries that repeat, and your meals that repeat (the Meals fractal's). Recurring budget entries are added to your calendar for you, like paychecks and bills, on every day they land on from today on. They start with a checkmark: ✔️ Regular, ✔️✖️ Hidden, or ✔️⭕️ Transfer. Help on the home page explains what each refraction type does.

Recurring Calendar Entries And Meal Entries

  • Recurring Calendar Entries lists your Calendar Fractals' entries that repeat. Tap one to change it or delete it (for every time it repeats), or tap New Calendar Entry to add one; it starts with Repeat on. Turn Repeat off, and it's on its first day only, so it leaves this list.
  • Recurring Meal Entries lists the meals that repeat, from the Recipes tab or New Entry's Meal. Tap one to change it or delete it, or tap New Meal Entry. A meal filled in from a recipe follows the recipe while its title, foods, and notes are left as the recipe has them.
  • To change just one day of either, tap it on that day on Home's calendar.

Adding And Changing Budget Entries

  • Tap New Entry to add one, or tap a row to change it or delete it. A title too long for its column ends in "..." (on a phone, after a second line): tap the title to see it whole, and tap it again to cut it short.
  • Placeholder makes every day it lands on a placeholder: a stand-in for an amount you expect, like an estimate for groceries. Its row and its days show in yellow, and each day's placeholder counts like any entry until that day passes, then it's deleted. With Placeholder on, Delete each one counts days from the current day: days behind the current day keeps each one until the current day is that many days past it (in your History too), and days ahead of the current day deletes them early, so the current day and the days after it, that many in all, don't have one (3 days ahead: today, tomorrow, and the day after); blank or 0 is once its own day passes. A day you change for it keeps its own Placeholder switch.
  • Wavelength (once Wavelength Planning is on, on Bob's page) is what its title's money is for, one of your wavelengths, which every entry with the title shares.
  • Remind Me sends a reminder that many days before each day it lands on (with reminders on in Settings), like an entry's own.
  • Add A Note, at the very bottom under the buttons, gives the recurring entry a note of its own, as long as you like, and underlines its title in the table (each day it lands on can have its own too: tap it on that day).
  • Two recurring entries can have the same title only when their refraction type or Equity account is different: Payday can be a Regular, a Hidden, and a Transfer recurring entry, and "Payday → Savings" and "Payday → HSA" are two different ones. If you add (or change) one so it matches another in title, type, and account, it says so, and you change the one that's there instead.
  • If two ever do match, they're listed at the top under "Needs a look": change one's title, type, or account, or delete one. Until then, on a day both land on, only the first one in the list does.
  • A Transfer with an account picked can come From another Equity account instead of In Bank, like moving money from Savings into an HSA every month: then In Bank stays the same.
  • For Hidden or Transfer, you can pick one of your Equity accounts, like Savings for a transfer into savings every payday. Each time it lands, a Transfer gives the account the opposite amount (a Transfer cost adds to it), and a Hidden entry its own amount (a Hidden cost takes from it). With an account picked, tap % next to the amount to make it a percent of the account's balance instead: each day it lands works out its own, from the balance as that day starts, and the table shows the percent. Help on the Equity page has more.
  • The little ? next to Refraction Type shows what each type does.
  • Its Days, in a recurring entry's window, lists every day it's on (on the calendar, in History, and in Upcoming), newest first. Tap one to change just that day.
  • To change just one day, tap that day on the calendar, then tap the entry. That makes a temporary recurring entry (like ❗️✔️) for that day only.
  • Changing a recurring entry's title or refraction type takes the days you changed for it along (from today on), so they keep their amounts, and a day you deleted it from stays deleted.

When They Land

  • Frequency is a number and Days, Weeks, or Months, like every 2 weeks. It counts from the Start Date: every 2 weeks lands on the Start Date, then 14 days later, and so on.
  • Months land on the Start Date's day of the month, or on the month's last day when the month is shorter (an entry that starts on the 31st lands on the 30th in 30-day months). Every 12 months is once a year.
  • For something on the 1st and the 15th, add two monthly entries with different titles, like Paycheck 1st and Paycheck 15th. (An older recurring entry set to Semi-Monthly still lands on the 1st and 15th.)
  • End Date is the last day it can land on. Leave it blank to keep it going.
  • Until $0 (under End Date, for a Hidden or Transfer entry with an Equity account, like a loan's payment) finds the day that account gets to $0.00 with everything planned, then ends the payments the day before and puts a last payment of only what's left on that day, so it lands on $0.00 exactly. It asks first.
  • Moving the End Date earlier turns any days you changed after it (from today on, like ❗️✔️) into plain unique entries, since there's nothing left for them to hold back; a $0.00 one goes away.

Days That Already Passed

Once a day passes, its recurring entries turn into unique entries (like ❗️✔️), so changing or deleting a recurring entry only changes today and later. Deleting one stops it from today on, and any days you changed for it keep their changes.

Monthly And Yearly

At the top, Monthly is about how much your recurring costs add up to each month (every 2 weeks counts as a little over twice a month). Yearly is what you've spent so far this year, plus every recurring cost still to land this year. Neither counts ⭕️ Transfers, since that money is still yours (✖️ Hidden costs count, like any cost).

Edit

Tap Edit to drag rows by their ≡ bar, add sections (+ Section) to group your rows, or check rows and delete them. A section's heading brings its whole section with it (its rows, up to the next heading), between two other sections or to the end.
