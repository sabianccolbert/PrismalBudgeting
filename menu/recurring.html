Recurring Entries Help

Recurring entries are added to your calendar for you, like paychecks and bills, on every day they land on from today on. They start with a checkmark: ✔️ Regular, ✔️✖️ Hidden, or ✔️⭕️ Transfer. Help on the home page explains what each refraction type does.

Adding And Changing

  • Tap New Entry to add one, or tap a row to change it or delete it. A title too long for its column ends in "..." (on a phone, after a second line): tap the title to see it whole, and tap it again to cut it short.
  • Placeholder makes every day it lands on a placeholder: a stand-in for an amount you expect, like an estimate for groceries. Its row and its days show in yellow, and each day's placeholder counts like any entry until that day passes, then it's deleted (so it never stays in your History). A day you change for it keeps its own Placeholder switch.
  • Two recurring entries can have the same title only when their refraction type or Other Account is different: Payday can be a Regular, a Hidden, and a Transfer recurring entry, and "Payday → Savings" and "Payday → HSA" are two different ones. If you add (or change) one so it matches another in title, type, and account, it says so, and you change the one that's there instead.
  • If two ever do match, they're listed at the top under "Needs a look": change one's title, type, or account, or delete one. Until then, on a day both land on, only the first one in the list does.
  • For Hidden or Transfer, you can pick one of your Other Accounts, like Savings for a transfer into savings every payday. Each time it lands, a Transfer gives the account the opposite amount (a Transfer cost adds to it), and a Hidden entry its own amount (a Hidden cost takes from it). With an account picked, tap % next to the amount to make it a percent of the account's balance instead: each day it lands works out its own, from the balance as that day starts, and the table shows the percent. Help on the Other Accounts page has more.
  • The little ? next to Refraction Type shows what each type does.
  • To change just one day, tap that day on the calendar, then tap the entry. That makes a temporary recurring entry (like ❗️✔️) for that day only.
  • Changing a recurring entry's title or refraction type takes the days you changed for it along (from today on), so they keep their amounts, and a day you deleted it from stays deleted.

When They Land

  • Frequency is a number and Days, Weeks, or Months, like every 2 weeks. It counts from the Start Date: every 2 weeks lands on the Start Date, then 14 days later, and so on.
  • Months land on the Start Date's day of the month, or on the month's last day when the month is shorter (an entry that starts on the 31st lands on the 30th in 30-day months). Every 12 months is once a year.
  • For something on the 1st and the 15th, add two monthly entries with different titles, like Paycheck 1st and Paycheck 15th. (An older recurring entry set to Semi-Monthly still lands on the 1st and 15th.)
  • End Date is the last day it can land on. Leave it blank to keep it going.
  • Moving the End Date earlier turns any days you changed after it (from today on, like ❗️✔️) into plain unique entries, since there's nothing left for them to hold back; a $0.00 one goes away.

Days That Already Passed

Once a day passes, its recurring entries turn into unique entries (like ❗️✔️), so changing or deleting a recurring entry only changes today and later. Deleting one stops it from today on, and any days you changed for it keep their changes.

Monthly And Yearly

At the top, Monthly is about how much your recurring costs add up to each month (every 2 weeks counts as a little over twice a month). Yearly is what you've spent so far this year, plus every recurring cost still to land this year. Neither counts ⭕️ Transfers, since that money is still yours (✖️ Hidden costs count, like any cost).
