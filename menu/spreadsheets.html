Connected Spreadsheets

Keep entries in your own spreadsheet (Google Sheets, Excel, and more)? Connect it, and they land on your calendar from today on, behind a ➕ (➕❗️ Regular, ➕✖️ Hidden, ➕⭕️ Transfer), following the spreadsheet as it changes.

    Share spreadsheets with admin@prismalbudget.org from a Google account with another email? Add that email in Settings, under Email (Other Emails), and open the link we send it. Then the spreadsheets it shares can be connected too.

    Connected Calendars

    Keep your work shifts on a calendar (Apple, Google, Outlook, or a work scheduling app)? Connect it with your pay, and every paycheck its shifts come to lands on your calendar on its payday, behind a ➕, worked out like a paystub (overtime, night rates, deductions, and taxes), and following the calendar as shifts are added, moved, or deleted.

    Connecting a calendar needs a verified email too: see the note under Connected Spreadsheets.

      Spreadsheets & Calendars Help

      Connect a spreadsheet you already keep, like a list of bills or paychecks, and its entries land on your calendar by themselves, from today on, like recurring entries. They start with a ➕: ➕❗️ Regular, ➕✖️ Hidden, or ➕⭕️ Transfer. Help on the home page explains what each refraction type does.

      1. Verify your email first. We email a link when you make your account (it works for 24 hours), and Send Verification Email (on this page, or in Settings under Email) sends a new one. If it isn't in your inbox, check your spam folder.
      2. Share the spreadsheet one of two ways: "Anyone with the link" can view it, or add admin@prismalbudget.org as a viewer (Google Sheets and Google Drive). For the second, share it from the Google account that uses your Prismal Budget email, or one whose email you added under Other Emails (in Settings, under Email), so only you can connect it.
      3. Tap Add Spreadsheet, paste its link, and tap Open Spreadsheet.
      4. For each part of an entry, tap the yellow ➕ to pick its cells: a tab (from the list, or type its name), then column and row, like column B row 2 to column B row 50 (that's B2:B50). Leave "to" empty for just one cell.
      5. Only the dates have to come from the spreadsheet. The title, amount, refraction type, and Other Account can come from cells too, or be typed in once for all of them. One cell works for all of them as well.
      6. The title and the Other Account are separate, like on the calendar: an entry reads "Rent → Savings", or "→ Savings" with no title. Each can come from its own cells, be typed in, or be left out (but not both).
      7. Check what it found under Entries, then tap Save.
      • Pick more rows than you use: empty rows are skipped, so entries you add later are picked up.
      • Amounts in cells: As Written reads -25 as a cost and 25 as a gain. Most bill lists write costs without a minus sign, so pick All Costs for those (or All Gains for paychecks). A cell that's a percent (like 3%) makes the entry a percent of its Other Account's balance as its day starts, so its entry needs an Other Account; a typed amount can be a % too, with the $ / % switch, once an account is picked.
      • Refraction types in cells: Regular, Hidden, or Transfer (an empty cell is Regular). Other Accounts in cells: the account's name, like Savings (an empty cell is none). If you rename an account, a spreadsheet with its name typed in follows it, but cells that name it need the new name.
      • Amounts can be up to $1,000,000,000, and titles up to 100 characters.
      • To keep the rest of a spreadsheet private, make a separate spreadsheet with only what Prismal Budget needs, filled from the first one (in Google Sheets, with =IMPORTRANGE), and connect that one instead.

      Staying Up To Date

      • Your spreadsheets are read again each time you open Home or this page, so changes, new rows, and deleted rows show on your calendar from today on.
      • To change one entry for one day, tap that day on the calendar, then tap the entry. That keeps your change for that day (like ❗️➕) even when the spreadsheet changes.
      • Once a day passes, its spreadsheet entries stay as they were (like ❗️➕), even if the spreadsheet changes later or you delete it.
      • An entry with the same title and refraction type as a recurring entry on the same day is left out (a day has one of each), and the spreadsheet's box says so.
      • They count everywhere your budget looks ahead: In Bank, Lowest, the 28 days after the calendar, Search, reminders, and Bob's page.
      • Turn on Placeholder (under the parts) to make every entry it adds a placeholder, like for a list of estimates: they show in yellow, count like any entry until their day passes, then they're deleted instead of staying.

      Other Emails

      Keep your spreadsheets in a Google account with another email than your Prismal Budget account's? Add that email in Settings, under Email (Other Emails), and we email it a link (it works for 24 hours). Once you open it, spreadsheets that Google account shares with admin@prismalbudget.org can be connected to your account, and the email signs in to it too. You can add up to 5, and removing one stops both.

      On, Off, And Delete

      Tap a spreadsheet's name to change it. Turning one off takes its entries off your calendar until you turn it back on (days you changed keep their changes). Deleting one takes its entries off from today on; days you changed become plain entries, and days that passed stay as they were.

      Problems

      When a cell can't be read (like a date that isn't a date, or an Other Account you don't have), the box under that spreadsheet says which cell and how to fix it. The other entries still land.

      Calendars

      Connect the calendar your work shifts are on and fill in your pay, and each paycheck lands on your calendar on its payday, behind a ➕ (like ➕❗️ $950.00 Paycheck), at what it comes to after deductions and taxes.

      1. Verify your email first, like for a spreadsheet.
      2. Copy the calendar's subscription link. The ? next to Calendar link says where each calendar app keeps it: Apple's starts with webcal://, Google's is the Secret address in iCal format, and Outlook's is a published calendar's ICS link. Most work scheduling apps have one too.
      3. Tap Add Calendar, paste the link, and tap Open Calendar.
      4. Under Pay, pick the first paycheck to count and how often you're paid, then fill in your pay, deductions, and taxes. A crystal from the Paycheck Calculator fills in the boxes it has.
      5. Check the paychecks it finds under the form, then tap Save Calendar.
      • Shifts: every event with a time is a shift (all-day events aren't, except bonuses), unless Only events with or Leave out events with says otherwise. Events that overlap count once. A shift counts on the day it starts, and one over 24 hours is a piece on each day (so each can be in a different paycheck). Or a shift past midnight can be split at midnight instead, so it can be in two paychecks.
      • Paydays: every week, every other week, twice a month, or once a month, and a payday on a weekend can be paid the Friday before or the Monday after. Each paycheck pays the full workweeks before its payday's week, or the days up to a few days before its payday.
      • Overtime: hours past the weekly limit (and a daily limit and double time, for places that have them), paid on the week's blended rate (the law's regular rate) or on each hour's own rate.
      • Pay By Event Title: a shift whose title has a rule's words in it is paid the rule's way: its own hourly and night rates, an extra per hour, a multiplier, a stipend for each shift (taxed, or tax-free like a per diem), its own unpaid break, or as paid time off (paid, but not counted toward overtime), on the days you pick.
      • Unpaid breaks come out of each shift that's long enough, and night pay is paid in the night hours you pick.
      • Bonuses: an all-day event with the bonus word and an amount, like Bonus $25, is paid in that week's paycheck.
      • Pay changes: a raise, a new deduction, or anything else that changes from one paycheck on. Tap Pay Change, pick the day it starts, and fill in only what's new. Paychecks before it stay as they were.
      • Taxes: FICA, then federal and state tax as a percentage, or worked out from your W-4 (federal) and from Oklahoma's withholding tables. Overtime can be left out of federal or state tax, all of it or just its extra over straight time. These are estimates: your employer's payroll decides your real paycheck.
      • Look ahead: paychecks paid further past today aren't added yet, since a schedule that far out may not be done.
      • Paychecks And Shifts (under each calendar) shows this year's take home, gross, taxes, and hours, the next paycheck, four weeks of shifts with their paydays, and every paycheck with hours. Tap a paycheck to see how it adds up, shift by shift.
      • Calendars are read again when you open Home (at most every 15 minutes on each device) or this page, so added, moved, or deleted shifts change your paychecks from today on. Like a spreadsheet's entries, a paycheck stays as it was once its day passes, one you change for its day keeps your change, and Placeholder makes them all placeholders.
      • Anyone with a calendar's link can see its events, so a calendar with just your shifts keeps the rest of your days private.
