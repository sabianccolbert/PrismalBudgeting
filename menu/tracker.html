Tracker Help

The tracker adds up your entries over the last 4 weeks, 3 months, and 365 days. It counts today and every day before it, from the calendar and History, so an entry counts as soon as its day comes.

Rows And Aliases

  • Each row counts the entries its aliases point to. An alias is an entry title, set to count All of its amounts, only its Costs, or only its Gains.
  • In a row's panel, each alias has its own line: type the entry title, then pick All, Costs, or Gains. Typing a comma (or pressing Enter) starts the next one.
  • Rows leave out ⭕️ Transfers, since most transfers aren't real costs or gains. Turn on Also count ⭕️ Transfers for a row that should count them (its title then starts with ⭕️).
  • ✖️ Hidden entries count like any other entry.
  • Entries for your Other Accounts (like a transfer into Savings) only move money between your own accounts, so they count like transfers, even when they're Hidden.
  • If more than one row has the same alias, each of those rows counts it.
  • To see the entries behind a row, search for its title with its Tracker button on the home page.

Costs, Gains, And Undefined

  • Costs and Gains add up every cost and every gain, except transfers and entries for your Other Accounts. They fill in by themselves.
  • Undefined lists the entry titles that no row counts yet, and adds them up. If you add an alias to a row but Undefined still lists it, one of two things is happening:
    1. The alias only counts Costs or only Gains, so entries of the other kind aren't counted yet. For example, if an alias for John counts Gains, costs titled John are still undefined.
    2. Some of those entries are ⭕️ Transfers (or entries for your Other Accounts), and the row doesn't count transfers. Turn on Also count ⭕️ Transfers for that row.

Changing Rows

Tap New Entry to add a row, or tap a row to change or delete it. Tap Edit to drag rows by their ≡ bar, add sections (+ Section), or check rows and delete them. Costs, Gains, and Undefined come back if you delete them.
