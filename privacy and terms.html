Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sabian Colbert (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Prismal Budget website and services (the “Site”). If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the Site.

Prismal Budget is intended for people 18 and older. You must be 18 or older to create an account (see Section 7).

1) What We Collect

A) Information You Provide directly

When you create an account, we collect the username, password, email address, and birth date you provide, and we record when you agreed to this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use. We ask your birth date only to make sure you're 18 or older; if you typed it wrong, you can fix it in Settings. We store your account details and your budget and finance data (such as your entries, recurring payments, history, and change logs) in our Cloudflare SQL database. We never store your password itself, only a protected one-way form of it (see Section 5). You can sign in with your username or your email address. We use your email address to send you account emails, such as email verification links, password reset links, and security notices. When you create your account or change your email, we email you a link to verify that the address is yours, and we record when it's verified. Connecting a spreadsheet requires a verified email (see Section 1E).

B) Passkeys (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello)

Passkey sign-in is optional. If you turn it on, your device (or the password manager it uses, such as iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager, or Windows Hello) makes a passkey for the Site, and we store the passkey's public key and ID, the name you give it, the site address it was made on, when it was added and last used, whether your device reports it as synced, and a sign-in counter. We also store a random ID for your account that your devices keep with its passkeys, and whether you chose to turn passkey sign-in on or off (so we only ask you once). The private part of a passkey never leaves your device or password manager. Your face, fingerprint, or device PIN is checked only by your device: we never receive, see, or store any biometric data, only a yes from your device that the check passed. Your device or password manager keeps the passkey along with your username (so it can show which account it's for), and those companies handle it under their own privacy policies.

C) Reminders, Notifications & Quick Entry

If you turn on daily reminders, we store your reminder time, your time zone, and which days your budget needs a reminder. If you turn on push notifications, we store the push address your browser gives us for that device (provided by your browser's push service, such as Apple, Google, or Mozilla). If you turn on a calendar link, we store the private code in that link. If you set up a Quick Entry home screen icon, we store a protected form of its key, along with the entries you make with it until they're added to your budget, and any prisms (saved entries you add often) you make on its page.

D) Analytics Data

We use Google Analytics to understand how the Site is used. It may collect general device and browser information, approximate location (derived from IP address), and site usage events. Google Analytics never sees your budget, and the page addresses we send it leave out everything after the page's name, such as the private code in a password reset link. It doesn't load at all if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, or if you turn analytics off for your device in Settings. Our infrastructure provider, Cloudflare, also counts page visits for us (Cloudflare Web Analytics); it doesn't use cookies or follow you across other websites. We don't show ads, and we don't use advertising services.

E) Connected Spreadsheets

Connecting a spreadsheet is optional, and requires your email address to be verified. If you connect one on the Spreadsheets page, we store its link, the name you give it, the names of its tabs, which cells each part of an entry comes from (or what you typed in instead), and the entries it makes (their dates, titles, amounts, refraction types, and Other Accounts), along with any problems found reading it, like a cell that isn't a date. Each time Home or Settings reads your spreadsheets, our server downloads each one from where it's kept (Google, Microsoft, or Dropbox) and passes the file straight to your browser, which reads the cells you picked. We don't keep the file, or any other part of the spreadsheet. The host receives that request from our server, not from your device, so it doesn't get your IP address from it.

You share a spreadsheet with us in one of two ways. Anyone with the link: anyone who has the link can see the whole spreadsheet, so we suggest a separate spreadsheet with only the cells Prismal Budget needs. Shared with admin@prismalbudget.org (Google Sheets and Google Drive): our Google account can view the spreadsheet, and we read it only for the Prismal Budget account whose verified email matches the Google account that owns it or shared it, never for anyone else, and only the way described above. You can remove our access at any time in the spreadsheet's Share settings. Deleting a connection in Settings deletes everything we stored about it, except the entries already on days that passed, which stay part of your budget.

F) Sign-Ins & Security Records

Each time you sign in, we keep a record of that sign-in (when it started and when it ends) so that signing out ends it on our side too. Each one is deleted when you sign out, when it ends after 24 hours, or when you change your password or sign out everywhere. To protect accounts and the Site from abuse, such as password guessing and spam, we also briefly keep counts of certain actions, like failed sign-ins. When a count is for a network, we store a scrambled form of the IP address it came from, never the address itself, and each count is deleted within about an hour.

2) Cookies & Browser Storage

Cookies: The Site itself doesn't set cookies. When Google Analytics is on, it sets its own cookies for this Site (named _ga and _ga_ followed by an ID) to count visits and tell a new visit from a returning one; turning analytics off in Settings, or a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, keeps it from loading. Cloudflare may set a short-lived security cookie if it needs to check that a visit comes from a person. We don't use advertising cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser's settings, and the Site works without them.

Storage in your browser: The Site keeps your sign-in (a signed token that stops working within 24 hours) and a few conveniences, like your Undo history (only in the open tab) and whether you've hidden the Getting Started card, in your browser's storage on your device. Signing out removes them, except your device's own choices (whether analytics is off, and the date format you picked).

3) How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

• operate and improve the Prismal Budget platform
• securely sync and display your personal budget data
• sign you in with your password or a passkey
• send account emails (such as email verification links, password reset links, and security notices when your password or email changes or a passkey is added) and the reminders you turn on
• troubleshoot issues, prevent fraud, and maintain security (like limiting password guesses)

We do not sell your personal financial data or budget information to anybody. Your data is strictly used to provide the budgeting service to you.

4) Sharing & Third Parties

We share information with service providers only as needed to operate the Site. This includes our website host (GitHub Pages), our server and database infrastructure provider (Cloudflare), our email delivery provider (Resend), which receives your email address and the contents of the emails we send you, your browser's push notification service (such as Apple, Google, or Mozilla) if you turn on push notifications, the services that keep any spreadsheets you connect (such as Google, Microsoft, or Dropbox), which receive our server's requests for them (see Section 1E), and our analytics provider (Google Analytics). These third parties process data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

If you bring a budget over from the Google Sheets version of Prismal Budget (a temporary option at the bottom of Home), you share that spreadsheet so anyone with its link can view it, and give us the link. Our server then downloads the spreadsheet's tabs from Google to turn them into your budget here. Google receives that request from our server, not from your device, so it doesn't get your IP address from it, and nothing else about you is sent. What comes over is stored like the rest of your budget data. Once it's done, you can turn the spreadsheet's link sharing back off in Google Sheets.

When you pick a new password, our server checks it against Have I Been Pwned's list of passwords exposed in data breaches. It sends only a small, scrambled piece of information that hundreds of different passwords share, so the service never receives your password, your username, your email address, or your IP address.

5) Security & Data Retention

We have reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational security measures in place to protect your account and your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, and alteration, and we review them as the Site changes. Your financial data and account details are stored with our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) and sent over encrypted connections. Your password is never stored in plain text, and we keep only the public part of a passkey, which can't be used to sign in. Account emails tell you when your password or email changes or a passkey is added, and in Settings you can sign out every device at once. Changing your password in Settings signs out your other devices and stops your Quick Entry icons; resetting it with an emailed link signs you out on every device, stops your Quick Entry icons, and turns passkey sign-in off until you turn it back on in Settings. To keep these protections effective, we don't publish the details of how they work. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. We retain your data as long as your account is active or as needed for legitimate business purposes. When you delete your account, we delete it and all of its data from our database right away, though copies may remain in our database provider's backups for up to 30 days.

6) Your Choices

• Account Data: You may update or delete your budget data on the Site. In Settings, you can change your email address (and verify it) or your password, fix your birth date if you typed it wrong, or delete your account and all of its data at any time.
• Connected Spreadsheets: On the Spreadsheets page (in the menu), you can turn each connected spreadsheet off or delete it at any time. To stop us from reading one at all, turn off its link sharing, or remove admin@prismalbudget.org, in the spreadsheet's Share settings.
• Download Your Data: In Settings, Download My Data gives you a copy of everything stored for your account (your budget, settings, and account details) as a JSON file, at any time. Passwords, keys, and private link codes are left out.
• Analytics: In Settings, you can turn Google Analytics off (or back on) for your device. Browsers that send a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal never load it.
• Signed-In Devices: In Settings, you can sign out every device at once, or turn off your Quick Entry icons.
• Passkeys: In Settings, you can turn passkey sign-in on or off at any time, add passkeys, or remove them. Removing a passkey deletes it from our database (you can also delete it from your device or password manager), and deleting your account deletes all of its passkeys.
• Reminders: You can turn push notifications and the calendar link on or off in Settings.
• Cookies: You can block or delete cookies in your browser's settings (see Section 2).
• Quick Entry: A Quick Entry icon can only add entries to your budget. It can't see your budget, only your Other Accounts and their balances (so you can pick one) and the prisms (saved entries you add often) you make on its page, which it can also change or delete. To stop all of your Quick Entry icons from working, turn them off in Settings or change your password.

7) Ages 18 And Older

Prismal Budget is intended for people 18 and older, and isn't for children. Creating an account asks for your birth date, and an account can't be made for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, contact us (see Section 8) and we'll delete it.

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to admin@prismalbudget.org.
