Add This Page To Your Home Screen

iPhone or iPad

In Safari, tap Share (on newer iPhones it can be under ⋯ first), then Add to Home Screen, then Add. Chrome on an iPhone works the same way: Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Samsung Galaxy

In Samsung Internet, tap ☰ (bottom right), then Add page to, then Home screen. In Chrome, tap ⋮ (top right), then Add to Home screen, then Add.

Google Pixel, OnePlus, or Motorola

In Chrome, tap ⋮ (top right), then Add to Home screen, then Add (if it asks, pick the shortcut). Touch and hold the new icon to move it where you like.

Xiaomi, Redmi, or POCO

In Chrome, tap ⋮ (top right), then Add to Home screen, then Add. If no icon shows up, let Chrome add one: Settings, Apps, Manage apps, Chrome, Other permissions, Home screen shortcuts (Allow), then try again.

Another Android phone

In Chrome, tap ⋮ (top right), then Add to Home screen. In Firefox or another browser, look for Add to Home screen in its menu.

A computer

Bookmark this page (Ctrl+D, or ⌘D on a Mac), or keep it in your bookmarks bar for one click.

Waiting Entries

Entries added here that haven't joined your budget yet. The next time Prismal Budget opens on the device that made this icon, they're added; opened on another device first, they wait on its Home, where whoever has it open adds them or deletes them. Until then, you can change or delete them here.

    • Prisms are entries you add often, saved so you don't have to type them each time. A prism can keep any of these: a title, an amount, a refraction type, and an Other Account. It can keep Placeholder turned on too (its button is yellow).
    • Add Prism turns New Entry into a prism. Fill in or pick only what it should keep (what's already in New Entry counts too), then tap Save Prism. A prism never keeps a date.
    • Tap a prism to put what it keeps into New Entry. Then fill in the rest, like the amount, or just tap Add Entry.
    • Edit Prisms lets you drag prisms into order by their ≡, or delete them with ×.
    • Prisms are saved with your budget, so every Quick Entry icon you make has the same ones.

      Quick Entry Help

      This page adds entries to your budget with no login: the next time Prismal Budget opens on the device that made this icon, they're added, and opened on another device first (like by someone you share it with), they wait on its Home, where whoever has it open adds them or deletes them. Until then, Undo here can take one back, and Waiting (next to Undo) lists the ones you added here, to change or delete. At the top, In Bank today and the lowest it gets (and on what day) count the entries added here too. Placeholder (for a day from today on) adds one as a placeholder: a stand-in for an entry you expect, like an estimate, shown in yellow. It counts like any entry until its day passes, then your budget deletes it. With Hidden or Transfer and an Other Account picked, the $ / % switch makes the amount a percent of that account's balance, which your budget works out for its day. Once the Needs, Wants, And Future card is on (on Jeremy's page), the form asks that at the bottom; this page can't see which one a title has, so nothing's picked to start, which keeps the title's as it is. Settings (Signed-In Devices) can turn those, and the Other Accounts' balances, off for every icon. This page can't see the rest of your budget. If your password changes, or icons are turned off in Settings, it stops working.

      One Tap From Your Lock Screen

      iPhone

      1. Copy this page's link:
      2. In the Shortcuts app, tap +, add the Open URLs action, paste the link into it, and name the shortcut (like Quick Entry).
      3. Touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, then Lock Screen. Tap one of the two buttons at the bottom (like the flashlight), remove it, tap + and pick Shortcut, then your shortcut (iOS 18 or newer). A Shortcuts widget under the clock works too.
      4. More ways to run it: an iPhone with an Action button (Settings, Action Button, Shortcut), or Back Tap, a double or triple tap on the back of the phone (Settings, Accessibility, Touch, Back Tap).

      Samsung Galaxy

      Samsung lets you pick the two Lock Screen shortcuts (Settings, Lock screen and AOD, Shortcuts), but they can only open apps, not a web page. Keep the Quick Entry icon in the row at the bottom of your home screen instead, for one tap right after unlocking.

      Google Pixel, OnePlus, Motorola, Xiaomi, or another Android phone

      Android's Lock Screen shortcuts are for things like the camera and flashlight, so they can't open a web page. Keep the Quick Entry icon in the row at the bottom of your home screen instead, for one tap right after unlocking.

      A computer

      Keep this page in your bookmarks bar for one click.

      In Bank On Your Watch

      Apple Watch

      Send this page's link to yourself in Messages (or email it to yourself), then open that message on your Apple Watch and tap the link: the watch shows In Bank today and the lowest it gets, with its day. Pin the conversation to find it fast next time.

      Other watches

      Most Wear OS watches (like Galaxy Watch and Pixel Watch) open links on the phone instead. If your watch has a browser app (like Samsung Internet for Galaxy Watch), open this page's link in it and it shows In Bank today and the lowest it gets.
