Add This Page To Your Home Screen

iPhone or iPad

In Safari, tap Share (on newer iPhones it can be under ⋯ first), then Add to Home Screen, then Add. Chrome on an iPhone works the same way: Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Samsung Galaxy

In Samsung Internet, tap ☰ (bottom right), then Add page to, then Home screen. In Chrome, tap ⋮ (top right), then Add to Home screen, then Add.

Google Pixel, OnePlus, or Motorola

In Chrome, tap ⋮ (top right), then Add to Home screen, then Add (if it asks, pick the shortcut). Touch and hold the new icon to move it where you like.

Xiaomi, Redmi, or POCO

In Chrome, tap ⋮ (top right), then Add to Home screen, then Add. If no icon shows up, let Chrome add one: Settings, Apps, Manage apps, Chrome, Other permissions, Home screen shortcuts (Allow), then try again.

Another Android phone

In Chrome, tap ⋮ (top right), then Add to Home screen. In Firefox or another browser, look for Add to Home screen in its menu.

A computer

Bookmark this page (Ctrl+D, or ⌘D on a Mac), or keep it in your bookmarks bar for one click.

Use the browser itself, not this page opened inside another app like Gmail, Instagram, or Facebook. If it's open inside one, use that app's menu (like ⋯) to open it in your browser first.

Then tap the new icon whenever you want to see your calendar or add to it. No login needed.

Quick Calendar Help

This page shows your calendar with no login, and adds entries to it. It never shows your budget.

  • Calendar Fractals (above the calendar) lists your calendars: check the ones to show.
  • Today, the month's ‹ and ›, the year, and the date box move the calendar. Days that passed are duller.
  • Tap a day to zoom in on it: its entries show beside the hours, or as a list with its List switch on. Swipe it left or right for the next day or the day before.
  • New Calendar Entry (or a zoomed day's +) adds an entry: its title, Calendar Fractal, when it starts and ends (or All day), travel time, where it is, whether it repeats, alerts, and a note.
  • Waiting entries (with a dashed edge) are the ones this icon added that haven't joined your calendar yet. They join the next time Prismal Planning opens on the device that made this icon (on any other device, they wait on Home to be added). Until then, tap one here to change it or delete it.
  • Entries already on your calendar can be looked at here, and changed after signing in.
  • The icon stops working when your password changes, on Sign Out Everywhere, or when Quick Calendar icons are turned off in Settings.
