Daily Reminder

On days your budget needs a look, you get a "🗓️ Check Budget" reminder at this time:

  • ⛔️ Your In Bank is negative that day
  • ✔️ An uncommon recurring payment (one that lands every 3 months or less often) is in the current four weeks
  • ❗️ You have a cost that day

It comes as a push notification, on your calendar, or both, whichever you turn on below. With both off, this goes away.

Push Notifications

A "🗓️ Check Budget" reminder on days your budget needs a look (like a day your In Bank goes below $0), as a notification on this device.

Language And Dates

Prismal Budget is in English, and your browser can show it in your language:

  • 🌐 Chrome or Edge: tap ⋮ (or right-click the page) and pick Translate.
  • 🧭 Safari: tap aA (or the translate button) in the address bar, then Translate.
  • 🦊 Firefox: tap the translate button in the address bar.

Your entries, names, amounts, and dates stay just as you typed them. Most browsers offer to translate on their own when a page isn't in your language.

Sounds

Soft crystal twinkles for what you tap, and music: a quiet bed of far-off twinkles in the background, with a clearer few now and then. They're for this device.

      Settings Help

      Your Account

      • Email: change it here (with your password). A new email gets a link to verify it, and until it's verified, spreadsheets can't be connected or read. Send Verification Email sends a new link.
      • Other Emails: add another email of yours (up to 5), and open the link we send it (it works for 24 hours). Once it's confirmed, you can sign in with it too (and use it for Forgot Password), and spreadsheets its Google account shares with admin@prismalbudget.org can be connected. Remove one to stop both. Adding one needs your own email verified first.
      • Quick Entry icons: turn off Show balances on Quick Entry icons, and they stop showing In Bank, the lowest it gets, and your Other Accounts' balances (just their names), on every icon.
      • Birth Date: Prismal Budget is for people 18 and older. If you typed the wrong birth date when you signed up, fix it here: type it, or pick it with the calendar button.
      • Change your password, add passkeys, turn off Quick Entry icons, sign out everywhere, download your data, or delete your account.
      • Upload A Backup: pick a file Download My Data saved, and for each part of your budget it has (History, Home's Calendar, Upcoming, Recurring Entries, Other Accounts, Goals, Bob's Page, Tracker, Connected Spreadsheets, Paycheck Calculator, Notes, and Change Logs), pick one: Prefer Current Data keeps yours where the two clash (like an entry with the same title and type on the same day) and adds the rest from the backup; Prefer Uploaded Data does the same, but the backup's wins where they clash; Remove Current Data puts the backup's in place of yours. Parts the backup doesn't have stay as they are. Undo can't take it back, so the window offers Download My Data first.
      • Sounds: turn Music (a quiet bed of far-off twinkles, with a clearer few now and then) and Sound effects (a soft crystal sound for what you tap) on or off for this device. They mix with your own audio instead of stopping it.
      • Connected spreadsheets have their own page: open the menu, then Spreadsheets.
