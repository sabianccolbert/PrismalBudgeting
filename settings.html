Budget And Calendar

Reminders

A "🗓️ Check Budget" reminder at a time you pick, on the days your budget needs a look. It can come as a notification on this device, as an event in your calendar app, or both. Turn one on, then pick what gets a reminder.

Notifications On This Device

Calendar Link

A private link that Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook, and most other calendar apps can subscribe to. Each reminder becomes a 30-minute event there, with an alert.

What Gets A Reminder

Turn on notifications on this device, or the calendar link, to pick what gets a reminder.

  • What they say: the title shows what it's about (like 🗓️🔔⛔️ Check Budget), and the rest says why. Only an entry's reminder names an entry (its title and day); the rest never show titles or amounts.
  • Notifications are turned on one device at a time, from that device (after a sign-in, Home alerts each device about its reminders once). On an iPhone or iPad, add Prismal Planning to your Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and turn them on there. Send Test sends one now.
  • The calendar link: Apple Calendar and Google Calendar open it with their buttons; in Outlook, use Add calendar, then Subscribe from web. Calendar apps check it on their own schedule (Google Calendar every several hours), so changes take a while there. Keep it private: anyone with it sees your reminder days. New Link replaces it.
  • Which days: worked out from your budget as it was the last time it was opened or changed on any device, so opening it now and then keeps them right.

Calendar Fractals' Links

A private link to your calendar's entries that another calendar app (Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook, and most others) or a spreadsheet can subscribe to: one for every Calendar Fractal together, or one for each. It carries each entry's title, time, location, notes, alerts, and how it repeats.

  • The other calendar shows your entries. Changes made there don't come back here.
  • Calendar apps check a link on their own schedule (Google Calendar only every several hours), so a change can take a while to show up there.
  • Keep a link private: anyone who has it can see those entries. New Link makes a new one (the old one stops working), and Turn Off ends it.

Sharing

Who each of your budgets and Calendar Fractals is shared with, and what they can do: turn each person's Can Edit on or off, take someone off, or share with someone new.

Kitchen

For the Kitchen tab, the Pantry, and the calendar's Meals fractal. These switches are this device's own, and each is off to start.

Reminders

  • Orange: the meals from today on use the Pantry up in order, the soonest first, so once a food runs out, only the later days that need it glow. The Kitchen tab's Help says how foods and units count.
  • Expiration days are only for their reminder: nothing else goes by them.

This Device

Theme

How Prismal Planning looks in this browser: its colors, corners, background, stars, and icons. Each browser keeps its own, even on the same device or budget.

Saved in this browser only. Every other place you use Prismal Planning keeps a theme of its own, so pick this one again in each:

  • Other browsers, on this device or another (like Chrome as well as Safari).
  • Quick Budget, Quick Calendar, and Snapshot icons already on a home screen. Each one is an app of its own, and keeps the theme it was added with: add it again (Home's Quick Budget or Quick Calendar button, or Snapshot's Add To Home Screen) for this one.
  • Prismal Planning added to a home screen as an app, which keeps its own as well (pick it in Settings there).

Custom makes one of your own, starting from any of the others. This browser keeps it, even when you sign out or pick another theme: tap Custom to bring it back, and tap it again while it's on to change it. The login page and the front page look like Prismal (the login page keeps your theme when Quick Budget's or Quick Calendar's Home opens it).

Language And Dates

Prismal Planning is in English, and your browser can show it in your language:

  • 🌐 Chrome or Edge: tap ⋮ (or right-click the page) and pick Translate.
  • 🧭 Safari: tap aA (or the translate button) in the address bar, then Translate.
  • 🦊 Firefox: tap the translate button in the address bar.

Your entries, names, amounts, and dates stay just as you typed them. Most browsers offer to translate on their own when a page isn't in your language.

Sounds

Soft crystal twinkles for what you tap, and music: a quiet bed of far-off twinkles in the background, with a clearer few now and then. They're for this device.

Reduced Motion

Less movement on this device, whoever signs in on it.

      Signed-In Devices

      Home Screen Icons

      Sign Out Everywhere

      Lost a phone, or signed in on a computer that isn't yours? This signs out every device and browser, this one too, and turns off your Quick Budget icons. Your budget stays just as it is.

      Your Data And Privacy

      Download a copy of everything stored for your account (your budgets, who they're shared with, settings, and account details) as a JSON file. Budgets other accounts shared with you are theirs, so they're only named. Passwords, keys, and private link codes are left out.

      Upload A Backup

      Bring a budget back from a file Download My Data saved, into the budget that's open (a file with more than one budget asks which). Part by part (History, Recurring Entries, Equity, and the rest), you pick what happens where the backup and the budget both have something. The file is read on this device.

      Analytics On This Device

      Delete Account

      This permanently deletes your account and everything in your budgets, for everyone they're shared with too. It can't be undone. What you added to budgets other accounts shared with you stays in them.

      • Home screen icons' pictures aren't in Settings: each Quick Budget, Quick Calendar, and Snapshot icon has its own, picked when it's made and changed with Customize at the bottom of its page.
      • Theme: how Prismal Planning looks in this browser. Other browsers, and apps on a home screen (Prismal Planning added there, and each Quick Budget and Quick Calendar icon), keep their own: once you pick one, the card lists where to pick it again. Tap one to switch, or Custom (at the end) to make your own: start from any theme, pick five colors (the background, the cards, the words, the glow, and what's chosen) and the corners, font, stars, and icons, and watch it change behind the window; Save Theme keeps it. The login page and the front page always look like Prismal.
      • Reduced Motion: Hold the starfield still keeps the stars where they are, and Turn off other animations stops pages sliding, windows growing out of their buttons, and days zooming, on this device. If your device asks for less motion in its own settings, both are on already.
      • Snapshot icons: Signed-In Devices says how many no-login Snapshot icons work (Snapshot's Add To Home Screen makes them). Turning them off stops every one right away.
      • Updates (at the bottom): the version you're on, and what each update changed, newest first, the bigger changes before the smaller ones. Its list scrolls.
      • Connected spreadsheets and calendars have their own page: open the menu, then Budgets, Calendars & Spreadsheets.
