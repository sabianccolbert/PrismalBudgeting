Your Cards

Snapshot Help

Snapshot is one page of the numbers and graphs you want to see, picked from across your budget. It starts with In Bank today, the lowest it gets, Upcoming, and the In Bank graph. Your cards are kept with your budget, so every device you use (and anyone you share it with) sees the same page.

Edit

  • Tap Edit to change the page, and Done when you're finished. Each change is saved right away.
  • + (at the top, and after the last card) adds a card there. Pick a part of the site, then what to show from it. A part with a › has more inside, like Bob's Goals (all of them, or one) or Data's numbers.
  • Hold a card's ≡ and drag it to move it, or focus it and use the arrow keys.
  • × takes a card off the page (after asking). It doesn't change your budget.
  • A Lowest In Bank After Today card has its own Until in Edit: as far as this device's Lowest looks (Home's Look Ahead), or a set time past the calendar, like 6 months. Each Lowest card keeps its own.
  • A Wavelength Planning card has its own Days: the past number of days, weeks, or months, since your last paycheck, or from one day to another. This device remembers each card's, apart from the card on Bob's page.

What A Card Can Be

  • Main Budget: In Bank today, the lowest it gets after today, In Bank at the last day worked out ahead, today's entries, Upcoming, and what Bob says on Home.
  • Bob: his highlights, the Spectrum Graph, your goals (all of them, or one), each of his cards, and Data (all of it, or one of its numbers).
  • Tracker: what a row came to in the last 3 months.
  • Equity: the Total, or one account today and 6 months ahead.
  • Recurring Entries: what they come to each month.
  • Gems: when an Accrual Calculator gem is paid off or reached (or, without a goal, what it grows to in a year).
  • Crystals: a Paycheck Calculator crystal's take-home pay, with the calculator's other boxes as they are.
  • Calendar: today's entries, or the next 7 days'.
  • Graphs: every graph on the site, including each tracker row's and each gem's. Each also has its own part of the site.

A card whose account, goal, gem, or row was deleted or renamed says so: take it off in Edit, and add it again. A card with More › opens what's behind it, the same as its page does.

Add To Home Screen

Add To Home Screen puts this page on your phone's home screen (iPhone, Samsung, Pixel, OnePlus, and more), so your Snapshot is one tap away. It starts with the icon's picture and colors (tap Next to keep them), then says what a no-login icon means, and gives two ways:

  • Make A No-Login Icon opens your Snapshot with no login, like Quick Budget's and Quick Calendar's icons. It shows this budget's cards, can read everything they're worked out from (the budget's entries, recurring entries, Upcoming entries, History, tracker, Equity accounts, goals, and calculators), and can't change anything. It never reads your Change Logs, notes, Calendar Fractals, or the links of your connected spreadsheets and calendars. Anyone holding the phone, or anyone its link reaches, can see your budget's numbers, so only make one on a phone you keep locked. Settings (Signed-In Devices) turns Snapshot icons off, and so do a new password and Sign Out Everywhere.
  • Keep The Login shows how to put this page on your home screen as it is: it asks you to log in, and after logging in it opens your Snapshot again.

A no-login icon's own page has Customize at the bottom, which changes that icon's picture and colors (an icon already on your home screen shows the new look once it's added again).
